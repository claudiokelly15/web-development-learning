JamboMall

JamboMall is a digital platform that connects merchants and customers anywhere across East Africa safely and quickly.

Features of JamboMall

  • secure payment protection for customers
  • package delivery tracking in real time
  • package delivery within 24 hours
  • Genuine product from verified registered agents
  • 24/7 customer support

How to Purchase Products

  1. Sign up for a JamboMall account.
  2. Search and select products you want to purchase.
  3. Contact the seller to inquire about the product details via whatsapp.
  4. Add products to your cart and proceed to checkout.
  5. Choose your preferred payment method and complete the payment.
  6. Track your package delivery in real-time until it reaches your doorstep.
