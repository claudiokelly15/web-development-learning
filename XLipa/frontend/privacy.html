Privacy Notice

This notice describes how XLipa collects, uses, shares, and protects information when businesses and their customers interact with XLipa services.

XLipa provides payment technology. Selcom processes and settles payments as applicable, and mobile money providers handle customer authorisation through their own services. Each provider may have its own privacy notice.

1. Scope and roles

This notice applies to information processed through XLipa websites, accounts, payment links, and integrations. XLipa supports businesses (“merchants”) that request payments. For merchant account and platform information, XLipa generally determines how the service uses that information. For payment processing, XLipa works with Selcom and relevant payment providers, who may handle information under their own roles and obligations.

If you are a customer paying a merchant, the merchant is responsible for its own customer relationship and privacy practices. Contact the merchant about the goods or services you bought.

2. Information we handle

The information depends on how you use XLipa and what is needed to provide the service. It may include:

  • Account and business details, such as name, contact details, business identity, ownership, and verification documents.
  • Payment information, including amount, currency, status, date, transaction reference, and relevant merchant or customer identifiers.
  • Technical and usage data, such as device and browser details, log events, security signals, and interaction with XLipa pages or integrations.
  • Support communications and information you choose to provide when contacting us.

Payment credentials such as a mobile money PIN are entered into the customer’s payment provider interface. Do not send PINs or passwords to XLipa support.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and administer accounts, assess business eligibility, create and track payment requests, reconcile transactions, support withdrawals, protect the service, prevent fraud and misuse, provide customer support, maintain records, improve reliability, and meet legal or provider requirements.

Where applicable, processing is based on providing the service you request, legitimate interests such as security and service improvement, consent where we ask for it, or legal obligations. We use information for purposes compatible with the service and explain significant changes to this notice.

4. When information is shared

We share only the information needed for the relevant purpose with:

  • Selcom and relevant mobile money, banking, or payment providers to process, settle, reconcile, or investigate transactions.
  • Service providers that support hosting, security, communications, analytics, and customer support under appropriate safeguards.
  • Authorities, regulators, courts, or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights, users, and the service.
  • A successor or involved party in a business reorganisation, subject to applicable protections.

We do not sell personal information. A merchant receives transaction details needed to identify and fulfil a customer payment.

5. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, maintain transaction and business records, enforce agreements, and meet legal or provider requirements. Retention periods vary by record type and circumstances.

We use administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards designed to protect information. No online service or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep account credentials and API keys private, and report suspected compromise promptly.

6. Your choices and rights

You can review and update many account details in your XLipa account or by contacting support. Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or an objection to certain processing. Some transaction or verification records may need to be retained or shared with payment providers even after an account closes.

To make a privacy request, email support@xlipa.net from an address associated with your account. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the request before acting.

7. Cookies and similar tools

XLipa pages may use essential browser storage or similar technologies to support core site functions, security, and preferences. If analytics or optional technologies are introduced, we will provide any choices or notices required by applicable law. You can manage cookies through your browser, though blocking essential storage may affect site functionality.

8. Processing locations

XLipa and its service providers may process information in Tanzania or other locations where service providers operate. Where information is transferred across borders, appropriate safeguards will be applied as required by applicable law. Contact us if you need more information about a specific transfer.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this notice as the service, providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the current version here and update the review date. Material changes may also be communicated through the service where appropriate.

Privacy questions or requests: support@xlipa.net · Contact XLipa
