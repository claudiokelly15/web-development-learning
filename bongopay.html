BongoPay

BongoPay is a payment gateway that allows users to make secure online payments. It supports various payment methods including credit cards, mobile money, and bank transfers.

Features

  • Secure payment processing
  • Multiple payment options
  • Easy integration with websites and apps
  • Real-time transaction tracking

How to Use BongoPay

  1. Sign up for a BongoPay account.
  2. Log in to your BongoPay account.
  3. Integrate BongoPay into your website or app using the provided API.
  4. Choose the payment method you want to use for transactions.
  5. Process payments securely and track transactions in real-time.
