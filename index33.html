Payment handling

Who handles your money

XLipa is a technology service, not a bank. It gives your business tools to request and track payments. Selcom processes and settles payments, while your customers approve transactions through their own mobile money service.

Each transaction is recorded with a unique reference in your XLipa account. Keep that reference handy if you need help reviewing a payment.

Help when you need it

Common questions

Quick answers to help you get started with XLipa payments.

How long does account review take?

Review time depends on the information provided. Submit complete and accurate details, and check your account for any follow-up request.

What if the customer does not receive a prompt?

Ask them to confirm the phone number and check their mobile network, then try the payment again from the same request.

How can I withdraw my balance?

Use the withdrawal options shown in your XLipa account and check the details there before submitting a request.

Can I connect my website or app?

Yes. You can integrate XLipa with a website or app using the developer documentation and the integration options available to your account.
