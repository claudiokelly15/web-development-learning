Payment handling

Who handles your money

XLipa provides the technology to request and track payments; it is not a bank. When a customer approves a payment, Selcom processes and settles it. The customer confirms the transaction through their own mobile money service.

Each transaction is recorded in your XLipa account with a unique reference. Keep that reference if you need help checking a payment.

Help when you need it

Common questions

Practical answers for setting up an account and handling a payment.

How long does account review take?

Review time depends on the information you provide. Submit complete, accurate details and check your XLipa account for any follow-up request.

What if the customer does not receive a payment prompt?

Ask them to confirm the phone number and check their mobile network. Before retrying, check the payment status in your XLipa account to avoid sending a second request for a payment that has already gone through.

How do I withdraw my balance?

Open the withdrawal options in your XLipa account, choose an available destination and review the amount and destination details before submitting your request.

What information helps support check a payment?

Share the transaction reference, amount and approximate time of payment. Never send your mobile money PIN when asking for help.
