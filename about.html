About XLipa

Payment tools for businesses ready to grow.

XLipa helps businesses in Tanzania collect mobile money payments through shareable payment links and online integrations. It also gives you a place to follow payment activity and references.

Why XLipa exists

Make it easier to get paid for the work you do.

Businesses often need a clear way to ask customers to pay, confirm what happened, and keep useful records. XLipa brings these steps into one payment experience, whether you share a link or connect a website or app.

Customers pay using supported mobile money options, and you can review payment activity in your XLipa account.

Payment handling

Who handles your money

XLipa is a technology service, not a bank. It gives your business tools to request and track payments. Selcom processes and settles payments, while your customers approve transactions through their own mobile money service.

Each transaction is recorded with a unique reference in your XLipa account. Keep that reference handy if you need help reviewing a payment.
