1. XLipa is a technology service

XLipa provides tools that help businesses request and track customer payments through payment links and online integrations. XLipa is not a bank, does not accept deposits, does not pay interest, and does not provide loans or credit. An XLipa account is not a bank account.

2. Payment providers

Selcom processes and settles payments as applicable to the service. Customers approve transactions through their own supported mobile money or payment provider. Those providers operate their own networks, systems, terms, and customer support processes. XLipa does not control their independent decisions, availability, or processing timelines.

3. Transaction outcomes and timing

Submitting a payment request does not guarantee authorisation, completion, or settlement. Network conditions, customer approval, provider rules, account review, and other factors may affect whether or when a transaction completes. A pending status is not proof of payment. Merchants should check the final status and reference in their XLipa account before fulfilling an order.

Balances, status displays, estimates, and transaction records are provided to help users follow activity. They may be subject to reconciliation with provider records, correction, reversal, or delay where appropriate.

4. Merchant responsibility

Merchants are responsible for the products or services they offer, accurate descriptions and prices, delivery, refunds, customer support, taxes, licences, and compliance with applicable laws. XLipa does not endorse or guarantee a merchant, listing, product, or service simply because it is paid for through XLipa.

Customers should contact the merchant about an order, refund, or dispute concerning the underlying purchase. Contact XLipa about a payment reference or a problem accessing XLipa features.

5. Availability and information

We aim to provide reliable information and access but do not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free service. Features, supported methods, and availability can change and may differ by account, provider, or location. Public examples, help content, and estimates are general information and may not reflect every transaction.

6. External links and integrations

XLipa may link to or work with third-party sites, systems, or services. Those services are governed by their own terms and privacy practices. A link or integration is provided for convenience and does not mean XLipa controls or guarantees the third party’s content or performance.

7. Questions

For questions about XLipa services or a transaction reference, contact support@xlipa.net or visit the XLipa contact page. For a product or order issue, contact the merchant directly.
