What happens when someone pays you

Four steps, from sharing a link to having the money in your account. Most payments are done in under a minute, depending on how fast your customer enters their PIN.

  1. 1

    You share a link

    Create it once in your dashboard, then send it on WhatsApp, Instagram or by SMS.

    bongopay.net/link/neema-kitenge
  2. 2

    They get a PIN prompt

    Your customer types their mobile money number and their network sends a prompt to their phone.

    Prompt sent to 0754 *** 412
  3. 3

    They approve it

    When they enter their PIN, we confirm the payment with Selcom and add it to your balance.

    BPY-20261001-7F3C21A9 · paid
  4. 4

    You withdraw

    Send your balance to mobile money or your bank. We check each withdrawal before it goes out.

    Withdrawal to CRDB · TZS 470,000

The API

For shops, apps and systems that need payments built in. One request sends the PIN prompt. We call your webhook when the payment is paid or fails, signed so you can check it came from us. 

# Ask the customer to pay TZS 50,000
curl -X POST https://bongopay.net/api/v1/pay-via-mobile \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Asha Mwakyusa",
    "phone": "255754312412",
    "amount": 50000,
    "transaction_id": "ORDER-1042"
  }'

# Response
{ "payment_reference": "BPY-20261001-7F3C21A9",
  "status": "pending" }

Who handles your money

BongoPay is software made by Bongofey. The payments themselves are processed and settled by Selcom, a licensed payment service provider in Tanzania. We never ask for your Selcom details, and you never need to deal with them.

Every shilling that comes in or goes out is recorded against your account, and every payment has a reference number like BPY-20261001-7F3C21A9. If something looks wrong, send us that number and we can trace it.

Common questions

How long does it take to get approved?

We review new accounts before they can take live payments. It depends on how complete your documents are, so upload your NIDA and TIN when you sign up.

What if my customer doesn't get the PIN prompt?

Ask them to check the number and that their phone has signal, then try again from the same link. You only pay the fee on payments that go through.

How do I get my money out?

Request a withdrawal from your dashboard to mobile money or your bank. We check each one before sending it, and there's no withdrawal fee.

Can I connect my own website or app?

Yes. Create an API key in your dashboard and follow the developer docs. Payments and webhooks work the same way as payment links.
