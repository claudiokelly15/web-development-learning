Prohibited Businesses Policy

This policy describes activities that cannot use XLipa and activities that require approval or additional checks before payment acceptance.

Effective 10 October 2026Applies to merchants and transactions

Payment providers and applicable laws may impose additional restrictions. XLipa may decline a business or transaction even when it is not specifically named below.

1. How this policy works

This policy applies to businesses, their owners, products and services, and transactions processed through XLipa. It supplements the Terms of Service. Merchants must describe their business honestly and must not route prohibited activity through another business, person, payment link, or account.

2. Prohibited activities

You may not use XLipa to offer, promote, facilitate, or collect payment for:

  • Illegal goods, services, or activities under applicable law, or transactions intended to evade sanctions or lawful restrictions.
  • Fraud, scams, impersonation, deceptive fundraising, pyramid schemes, or misleading investment opportunities.
  • Weapons, ammunition, explosives, or other restricted dangerous goods where their sale or transfer is unlawful or unsupported by providers.
  • Controlled drugs, illegal substances, or products making unlawful drug or medical claims.
  • Counterfeit, stolen, or unlawfully obtained goods, or products that infringe intellectual property rights.
  • Child sexual abuse material, sexual exploitation, trafficking, or any service that exploits or endangers a person.
  • Unlicensed gambling, betting, lotteries, or games of chance, where a required licence or provider approval is absent.
  • Money laundering, terrorist financing, unlicensed money transmission, or the unauthorised collection or movement of funds for third parties.
  • Malware, credential theft, hacking services, or tools primarily intended to bypass security or enable unauthorised access.
  • Any activity that violates the rules of Selcom or a relevant payment method.

3. Restricted activities requiring prior review

Some lawful activities may carry additional legal, fraud, customer-harm, or provider risk. Do not accept payments through XLipa for these categories until you have disclosed the activity and received written approval from XLipa:

  • Regulated financial, lending, investment, insurance, or debt collection services.
  • Charities, political fundraising, donations, or collection of funds on behalf of other people or organisations.
  • Gambling, gaming, prize competitions, or betting that may be permitted only with a specific licence.
  • Alcohol, tobacco, nicotine, vaping products, or age-restricted goods and services.
  • Pharmaceuticals, telemedicine, health products, or medical services requiring professional licensing.
  • Digital assets, virtual currencies, foreign exchange, or high-risk trading services.
  • Subscription billing, recurring charges, advance payments, or high-value goods where the delivery or refund model needs review.

Approval may require licences, ownership information, product details, fulfilment policies, or other supporting documents. Approval can be limited to a specific product, channel, or payment method and may be withdrawn if circumstances change.

4. Merchant obligations

You must keep your business profile and product descriptions accurate, obtain all required licences, follow age and location restrictions, and retain records showing that orders were authorised and fulfilled. Do not disguise a restricted business, split transactions to avoid review, or use XLipa for personal or unrelated third-party payments.

Tell XLipa promptly if your ownership, products, delivery method, or business model changes in a way that could affect your eligibility.

5. Review and enforcement

XLipa may request information, decline onboarding, limit payment methods, delay or reject transactions, suspend payment links, or restrict or close an account when needed to manage risk, follow provider requirements, or comply with law. We may share relevant information with Selcom or other providers for their review. Where practical and lawful, we will explain next steps and provide a support contact.

This list is not exhaustive. Applicable laws, payment provider rules, and risk controls may prohibit additional activities or transactions.

6. Questions and disclosure

Before launching a restricted or unusual business model, contact support@xlipa.net with a clear description of the products, customer locations, licences, and payment flow. Do not accept payments for the activity while review is pending.
